      How to read this
      • The muscles and what they do come from the sources. Each muscle's attachments and the action credited to it are taken from the cited paper and paraphrased. The model file says where the muscle attaches; the page works out what shortening it does from the geometry.
      • The geometry is schematic. Outlines, lever arms and ranges of motion are illustrative. tests/sim.test.js checks that every drawn muscle moves its joints the way its source says, so a landmark cannot be moved for looks and quietly reverse a muscle. It does not make the drawing a measurement.
      • One muscle at a time is unopposed. Contracting a muscle on its own shows the direction of its pull from a starting pose, stopping at the joint limits. Real movements are made by several muscles at once.
      • Gaps are shown, not filled. A dot in the comparison table means the source table for that animal does not record the muscle. Absent means a source says it is missing. Muscles the dataset has parked, or has not yet scored for the animal, are marked in the detail card.
