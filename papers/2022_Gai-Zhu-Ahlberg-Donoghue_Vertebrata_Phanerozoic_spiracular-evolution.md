# Gai, Zhu, Ahlberg & Donoghue (2022) — Evolution of the spiracular region

## Citation

Gai Z, Zhu M, Ahlberg PE, Donoghue PCJ. 2022. The evolution of the spiracular region
from jawless fishes to tetrapods. *Frontiers in Ecology and Evolution* 10: 887172.

## What it gives

The fossil sequence for the spiracular region across the whole span from jawless
fishes to tetrapods — which is the anatomical basis for treating the **spiracular
pouch as antecedent to the tympanic cavity**.

## What it is used for here

Cited on two records as background, with no rows:

- **`stapedius`** — because the middle ear it sits in is a modified spiracular
  space, which is the other half of the hyomandibula-to-stapes story.
- **`levator-arcus-palatini`** — the spiracular series muscle. It is scored
  `present: "no"` in a human on the grounds that the series has no muscle there,
  and the note says the *region* persists even where the muscle does not. This
  paper is what licenses that distinction.

## Not mined

**No occurrence rows**, correctly: this is a comparative and palaeontological
synthesis, not a description of a muscle in an animal.

## Relevance to comparative anatomy teaching

The paper that makes the spiracle a through-line rather than a curiosity — the same
opening tracked from an agnathan through a shark to the middle ear of a mammal.
Pair with **Clack, Fay & Popper (2016)** on the ear and with **Miyashita (2016)** on
the mandibular arch, which is the neighbouring segment.
